# Publication readiness after iteration 13

**Iteration 13 update.** The methods manuscript now includes a pre-registered positive result (Section 8): relation reparameterization makes MedHyperDiff better than published generators at reproducing approximate laboratory relations on a fresh population, and at 250 training records also more realistic and useful. Strengthening options: replicate on a second fresh population with approximate relations (for example a different NHANES cycle) and with tuned comparator configurations.

**Iteration 12 update — methods paper.** The project's primary manuscript is now the methods paper [methods_manuscript.md](../paper/methods_manuscript.md): seven pitfalls, the case study and a pre-registered controlled study on fresh public populations with definitional relations, large reference sets, two attacks and ranking-reversal analyses. Its evidence no longer depends on the opened UCI test folds or on unreviewed clinical rules (NHANES identities are definitional). Remaining before submission: researcher declarations and authorship; optionally a third population or a second NHANES cycle, survey-weighted sensitivity, and tuned rather than default configurations in the controlled study; venue formatting and a final literature refresh.

**Iteration 11 update.** Training, representation and evaluation defects were corrected and a frozen, equal-budget confirmation was run on the reserved test folds of four cohorts against upstream-recipe TabDDPM and TabDiff, CTGAN, TVAE and statistical references (see [interpretation](../reports/iteration11/INTERPRETATION.md)). This closes the earlier gaps on stable external diffusion baselines, a frozen confirmatory evaluation, additional cohorts and wrong-score rule evaluation. The supportable claim is now scoped: large improvement over the previous model and the most faithful reproduction of supplied ILPD clinical relations, without superiority in overall realism (TabDiff and the copula have lower C2ST) or utility. Remaining before submission: clinician/laboratory review of the ILPD rules, cohorts with reviewed multi-feature relations, stronger privacy attacks (diabetes outputs of all diffusion models are memorized), more than one outer fold, and researcher declarations.

The project has a working, trained synthetic medical-table generator and reproducible development evidence. **It is not ready for a superiority or clinical-validity paper.** Successful code checks do not substitute for the scientific requirements below. The present evidence supports studying the tradeoffs of supplied constraints, missingness and distribution shape.

## Priority for the next experiments

1. **Keep the stronger references and complete external diffusion reproduction.** Iteration 7 completes current matched CTGAN/TVAE and statistical comparisons. Require both TVAE presets and explicitly repaired copula in future comparisons, alongside dense/backbone controls. The repaired copula already has better numeric/association fidelity and observed compliance; the method needs an advantage beyond supplied-rule repair. Diagnose unsuccessful external diffusion sampling and retain unsuccessful attempts. A failed external reproduction is not evidence that MedHyperDiff is better.
2. **Stop broad unbounded transform tuning.** The predeclared asinh-MAD representation was rejected because its inverse generated extreme bilirubin outliers and 21–25% supplied-rule violations. If a new numerical representation is proposed, require an explicit tail-stability or bounded-support rationale before fitting, verify physical-unit constraints and compare against the repaired copula.
3. **Close the clinical and generalization gaps.** Iteration 9 shows that omission/reversal of supplied relations materially changes compliance, and real validation violates the current A/G identity rule. Obtain clinician/laboratory review of units, timing, rounding and applicability; then choose additional cohorts with meaningful reviewed relationships, freeze a defensible confirmatory protocol and improve privacy evaluation before making submission-level claims.

These are research priorities, not completed experiments or a claim that any proposed change will work. Do not keep adjusting thresholds to make a favored model pass. The current engineering gates are descriptive development tolerances, not clinical noninferiority margins or formal hypothesis tests. Repeated development comparisons must be disclosed.

## Shape of the eventual paper

The main paper should state one precise contribution and show the final matched comparisons, central-versus-tail fidelity, utility and rule coverage together. The simulator mechanism test can isolate supplied structure. Move exhaustive development grids, failed settings, implementation audits and chronological experiment counts to appendices or a reproducibility supplement. Preserve them rather than discarding negative results. The current manuscript is a working record; it still requires consolidation once the final method and confirmatory experiment are fixed.
