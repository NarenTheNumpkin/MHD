# Iteration 5: train-only observation-head initialization and loss sensitivity

Written before fitting or sampling this stage. This exploratory ILPD experiment responds to iteration 4's repeated missing A/G-ratio excess in inner split 2027 / training seed 1. It tests a hypothesis about optimization and calibration; the previous results do not establish the cause of that failure.

## Frozen interventions and comparisons

Use the iteration-4 updated full velocity configuration (clinical weight 0.01, guidance 10), its matched dense control and the same-guidance velocity backbone. For observation heads with both states present in fit, initialize output weights to zero and biases to the log of empirical fit-state frequencies. Leave constant masks, ordinary categories, numerical heads and all shared weights unchanged. The probabilities initially equal unconditional fit frequencies, but all head weights remain trainable and may learn conditional dependence. This is an initialization experiment, not a guarantee of calibration. No validation labels/features determine these initial frequencies.

Fit four variants from scratch: full with prior initialization; full with prior initialization and observation-loss multiplier 4; dense with prior initialization; backbone with prior initialization. The observation-loss multiplier scales only the observation-token CE contributions inside the original fixed categorical-token denominator. It does not rebalance missing/observed classes or change support, numerical targets, clinical energy, learning rate or sampling. Constant observation tokens already have zero CE when their only observed state is allowed. Initializing rare heads is intended to avoid arbitrary initial rare-event logits; greater loss weight tests whether their reconstruction signal is too weak. Neither outcome is assumed in advance.

Three inner seeds (2027, 3027, 4027) × three training seeds (0, 1, 2) × four variants = 36 new generator fits. Use unchanged fit partitions, preprocessing, graphs, constraints and original training budgets from the corresponding iteration-4 parent. Reset the same paired training RNG and initialize the same shared parameters. The new fits do not warm-start trained weights. Checkpoint selection still uses each variant's fixed-corruption validation loss; different loss weights may select different epochs, which is part of the intervention.

Generate three pools per fitted model with seeds 70001, 70002, 70003, batch size 256 and five repetitions of the original fit-label vector: 108 new pools/evaluations. Use fixed evaluator seed 2026. Reuse the 81 existing 5x iteration-4 evaluations for unmodified full, dense and backbone models, with matching training/sampling seeds and label order. This yields 189 evaluation records referencing 63 fitted models, of which 36 are new. Using 5x pools here reduces rare-event count granularity; it does not change the proposed model's default or assert that 5x improves utility.

Outer fold 0 / split seed 2026 and original reserved test remain fixed. Do not compute reserved-test outcomes. The core package remains unchanged; experimental training logic is isolated in an archived script module, with equivalence tests for weight-1 loss and original checkpoint replay. No new external baseline claim is made.

## Outcomes and gates

Retain all fits and evaluations. Report per-feature fit/validation/synthetic missingness, rule coverage and unknown-rule violation bounds, forest/logistic TSTR and augmentation utility, numerical Wasserstein, association RMSE, per-feature variance ratios, discriminator and empirical privacy diagnostics. Average sampling seeds within each fitted checkpoint, then training seeds, separately by inner partition. Seeds and overlapping partitions are not independent patient cohorts; no formal population inference.

Compare each new variant with its unmodified architecture at identical seeds and 5x size. For full variants, a corrective candidate must remove every table-level feature missingness error greater than 0.02 versus fit, reduce the maximum such error across all 27 tables, and have no more than 0.02 AUROC decline for either classifier in any partition mean. Require per-partition numerical Wasserstein and association RMSE ≤1.1 times reference, observed violation and upper-bound violation increases ≤0.01, no rule-coverage decrease >0.01, no per-feature missing-error increase >0.01 versus fit or validation, and no increase >0.10 in any numerical feature's mean absolute standard-deviation-ratio error |SDsynthetic/SDvalidation−1|. These are engineering tolerances, not clinical thresholds.

Report prior-initialized full versus prior-initialized dense/backbone separately as structural controls. Weight-4 full is an optimization sensitivity comparison, not a parameter-and-loss-matched structural superiority test. If neither full variant passes all gates, do not promote or silently replace the existing sampler. If one or both pass, retain them as development candidates, rank by worst feature missingness error then mean Wasserstein, and make no publication or clinical validity claim. Correcting one missingness metric alone is insufficient; compressed numerical variability remains an explicit guardrail.
