# Iteration 12: what the controlled study shows

**Bottom line.** On two fresh public populations with definitional and approximate laboratory relations, twelve generator variants, two training sizes and five independent training samples per size (240 jobs, none failed), common training and evaluation practices change the ranking of generators substantially. Moving from a "common practice" pipeline to the corrected one reverses the order of **25–34% of generator pairs** (Kendall τ 0.33–0.50) and changes the top-ranked generator in **18 of 20 training samples**. Every pre-registered analysis was run; results follow, including those that weaken earlier claims. Tables: [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md). Protocol: [ITERATION_12_PROTOCOL.md](../../docs/ITERATION_12_PROTOCOL.md).

## Per pitfall

**P1 — checkpoint selection (strong).** With the original patience-20 validation-loss selection, 30% of MedHyperDiff fits kept a checkpoint from the first 20 epochs (as early as epoch 3) and 45% stopped at the 100-epoch cap. Relative to fixed-budget training on identical fit rows, C2ST was worse in all 20 paired samples (+0.22 and +0.16 on NHANES and thyroid at n = 250; +0.17 and +0.07 at n = 1,000), as was association error (+0.01 to +0.10). The penalty is largest for the smallest training sets.

**P5 — baseline recipe (strong).** The iteration-2 TabDDPM adapter loop (constant learning rate, batch 64, clipping, validation early stopping) on the same architecture as the upstream recipe produced numerical divergence: C2ST 0.995–1.000 and numeric Wasserstein +2.4 to +3.7 scaled units worse in all 20 samples, with forest utility 0.49–0.70 instead of 0.87–0.94.

**P2 — representation (moderate, context-dependent).** Quantile coding lowered C2ST relative to affine coding in three of four settings (all five samples each: −0.024 NHANES n = 1,000, −0.031 and −0.073 thyroid) but raised it for NHANES at n = 250 (+0.030, all five samples). It improved the most skewed hormone and enzyme distributions (TSH, GGT) but not glucose or creatinine. The case study's impression of a uniformly large effect is not supported; affine coding remains a reasonable alternative to test.

**P3 — recording resolution (strong, generator-specific).** Decoding to recorded resolution moved MedHyperDiff's C2ST from 0.979–0.999 to 0.692–0.731 on NHANES and from 0.749–0.808 to 0.685–0.707 on thyroid, and TVAE's from 0.97–0.99 to 0.91 on NHANES; TabDDPM and TabDiff hardly changed. The reason is an implementation detail: their scikit-learn quantile inverses interpolate between tied knots, so 45–98% of their raw values already fall on the recording grid, whereas MedHyperDiff, TVAE and CTGAN raw outputs are essentially never on-grid. Ranking by raw versus decoded C2ST reverses 29–32% of generator pairs on NHANES and 7–14% on thyroid, and changes the top generator (on NHANES n = 250, from copula re-derivation in four samples and TabDiff in one to MedHyperDiff's affine variant in all five). On thyroid, magnitude-banded decoding matters beyond a single grid because TSH is reported to about two significant figures (MedHyperDiff n = 1,000: 0.808 raw, 0.763 global, 0.685 banded).

**P4 — rule scoring (moderate).** Ranking by synthetic violation counts versus the gap to real violation rates reverses 11–22% of pairs, but not the winner: explicit re-derivation of the supplied relations is best under both, because every learned generator breaks them. On NHANES, where globulin and BMI are definitional, 90–100% of learned-generator rows violate at recorded resolution (real 0%); on thyroid the FTI relation is broken in most rows (real ≈5%). The over-correction failure mode of zero-violation scoring is the ILPD envelope case from the case study; here the practical lesson is that definitional variables should be re-derived, not generated, and envelopes scored against real behaviour.

**P6 — memorization (strong).** Across generators, lower C2ST goes with higher membership-inference AUROC (Spearman −0.33 to −0.77). Excluding the explicit copy control, the most realistic generator in a training sample exceeded membership AUROC 0.60 on at least one attack in 40% (NHANES n = 250), 0% (NHANES n = 1,000), 80% (thyroid n = 250) and 20% (thyroid n = 1,000) of samples; this includes corrected MedHyperDiff under the density-ratio attack on thyroid at n = 250 (mean 0.62). Upstream TabDDPM memorizes at n = 250 (0.85–0.89). In the case-study search, the lowest-C2ST configuration of a method memorized in 10 of 20 cohort-method cells. The density-ratio attack was usually more sensitive than nearest-distance matching.

**P7 — evaluation size (strong).** A single C2ST draw orders a generator pair as the full reference does with probability 0.77–0.78 at 50 records per source, 0.84–0.85 at 100, 0.88–0.90 at 200 and 0.93–0.94 at 500; the 90% level is first reached at 500. Pairs whose true C2ST differs by at most 0.02 are ordered correctly only 58–71% of the time even at 1,000. The standard deviation of a single draw falls from 0.062 at 50 to 0.008 at 1,000. Typical public clinical test folds of 60–120 patients therefore cannot separate competitive generators.

## Overall pipeline comparison

Under common practice (early-stopping diffusion training, the adapter TabDDPM recipe, raw outputs, C2ST on 100 records per source, no privacy check), the top-ranked generator was the copula with re-derivation, the copula or TabDiff, and the case-study model ranked 4th–6th of 8 (2nd–4th on thyroid n = 1,000). Under the corrected pipeline it ranked first in 16 of 20 samples. With privacy-aware ranking (memorizing generators last) the top generator on thyroid n = 250 becomes the copula with re-derivation. With fixed, untuned default configurations, corrected MedHyperDiff had the lowest C2ST of the non-copying generators in three of four settings (NHANES n = 1,000: 0.692 vs TabDiff 0.703; thyroid n = 250: 0.707 vs 0.742; thyroid n = 1,000: 0.685 vs 0.692) and its affine variant in the fourth; this is not a tuned model comparison, and the iteration-11 tuned comparison on four other cohorts favoured TabDiff. The lesson is the size of the ranking shifts, not a new winner.

## Limitations

Two populations, one NHANES cycle without survey weights, and fixed default configurations; replicates share a common pool and reference split, so intervals describe training-sample variation only. The 0.60 membership threshold is a convention; both attacks are empirical diagnostics without formal guarantees. MedHyperDiff ran without guidance here, so its rule behaviour reflects structure and energy training only.
